# Shoe Bot Update: Run It Back 👟

Three new features just landed. Your streaks now get a proper send-off, and your friends are officially competition.

**Streak Recap Cards**
When a streak breaks, get the full rundown: how many shoes you stacked, how long it lasted, how many people joined in, and how close you came to the server record.

**`/shoecheck text:...` — Test before you trip**
Not sure your creative spelling or emoji counts? Check it privately without risking the streak. Relay rules still apply when you actually post.

**`/rival user:@friend` — Settle the score**
Compare your Shoe totals, see who's ahead, and find out exactly how many accepted shoes you need to take the lead. Friendly competition. Extremely serious footwear.

Your existing counts and records carry over. Pick a rival, check your shoe, and **run it back.**
